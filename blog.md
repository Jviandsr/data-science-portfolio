Living and studying in Charlotte, NC has been an amazing experience. It's a bustling city with a fantastic tech community, calm avenues, and booming opportunities in analytics and tech.

---

## Why Data Science?

My drive to pursue data science comes down to two big things: **problem-solving** and **job security**. 

Data is everywhere, but turning raw, unstructured datasets into actionable, real-world insights is where the real magic happens. Whether it's analyzing publishing trends via the Open Library API, parsing machine learning models for NFL statistics, or building predictive models, I thrive on taking complex data and translating it into clear stories.

My goal is to build a long-term, resilient career in data analytics and machine learning—opening new doors for my family while contributing value to data-driven teams.
