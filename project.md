# Research Overview & Questions

> **Topic:** Online Book Marketplace Pricing Strategies & Consumer Engagement

---

### Research Questions

1. **Category Discount Variations**  
   *How do list price discounts on online platforms vary across different book categories/genres, and does higher discounting correlate with higher consumer rating counts?*

2. **Genre Reliance on Pricing**  
   *Which book categories feature the largest price reductions on online marketplaces, and do certain genres rely more heavily on steep online discounts to drive sales?*

---

### Planned Data Sources

| Platform | Primary Use Case | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Google Books API** | List pricing, sales capability, and review counts | [`Primary Candidate`](https://developers.google.com/books)
| **Open Library API** | Edition metadata and broader historical categorization | [`Secondary Candidate`](https://openlibrary.org/developers/api)

---

### Variables & Features to Investigate

*  **Primary Metric:** Discount percentage vs. consumer rating counts (proxy for sales volume).
*  **Categorical Metric:** Frequency and depth of discounts grouped by genre.
*  **Confounding Variable:** Genre popularity trends and seasonality.
